A game of tariffs: is there demand for tariffs in Europe?

Co-authors: Michal Grahn, Katharina Lawall, Maria Bergström Nordbrandt & Stuart J. Turnbull-Dugarte
Journal of European Public Policy, 2025 ⤴
Following the announcement of US tariffs on the EU and UK in April 2025, we use conjoint experiments in Germany and the United Kingdom to examine European preferences for tariff policies. Despite polling suggesting strong support for retaliatory measures, we find Europeans consistently reject tariffs when presented alongside alternative policies.
